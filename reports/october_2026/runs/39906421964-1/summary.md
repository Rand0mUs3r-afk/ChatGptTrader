# October 2026 entry-only backtest (fractional shares)

Only October 2026 entries are allowed, through the latest completed October session available; earlier bars are training-only. Newly selected TSX movers/ETFs are eligible from October 23, 2026, not retrospectively from October 1. Until that session completes they do not enter this backtest. Separate flat-start US/USD and TSX/CAD scenarios, one position at a time, no FX pooling or same-day reentry. Prior-close structural SL and next-open entry; TP and RR use only earlier bars and matured outcomes. Allocation cap 25%, planned-risk cap 1.5% of equity; 15 bps/side, zero commissions/dividend cash. Fractional mode uses 0.001-share steps and minimum notional 1; quantities and P&L retain fractions. Broker eligibility, precision and fractional SL/TP execution are unverified simulation assumptions. RR/evidence filters remain unchanged. Stop-first daily fills and gap-risk assumptions remain. Open positions are marked separately, not forced wins/losses. Current-universe/development-sample bias remains; this is not independent live validation and no future data is fabricated.

TSX additions not yet eligible: ABRA.TO, AFN.TO, ASTL.TO, FTG.TO, HPS-A.TO, MDA.TO, MTL.TO, VAB.TO, VCN.TO, VFV.TO, VIU.TO.
