# 2026 year-to-date entries - maximum 45 trading sessions (AMD excluded from US universe)

AMD is explicitly excluded from the US universe at the user request after an invalid October 23 Close. The US universe differs from earlier runs; this is not an identical-universe comparison. Shared repository watchlists and all other data validation and eligibility rules remain unchanged.

2026 YEAR-TO-DATE ENTRY RESEARCH; maximum 45 TRADING SESSIONS, entry is session one. Entries from January 1 through latest completed 2026 exchange session, never future prices. SL/TP may exit earlier; open positions are marked separately, not forced wins/losses. Up to ten years of pre-2026 history are requested for 45-session nonoverlapping calibration; later pre-entry matured 2026 outcomes may update the model causally. Same empirical target grid, 20 calibration/10 qualifying walk-forward minimums, positive mean net-R checks and 30-example training window as the 15-session experiment. Targets/stops are not mechanically scaled with holding time. Shorter-listed stocks may lack sufficient empirical evidence and are rejected, not granted looser thresholds. At least 60 pre-January bars are required for price-feature warm-up; exclusions are disclosed. This is a retrospective current-baseline-watchlist study, NOT a reconstructed point-in-time 2026 universe. Known later TSX additions retain their recorded eligibility dates, not January 1. Historical hit rates are descriptive, not forecast probabilities; old regimes and selection bias remain. Compression/balanced/trend-family signals, original pattern stops, empirical targets and labeled fixed-2R controls. Separate USD/CAD accounts, one position at a time, 25% allocation and 1.5% planned equity-risk caps, 15 bps/side; gaps can exceed planned risk. No commissions or dividend cash, daily stop-first fills, fractional broker/order eligibility unverified. Zero entries are not evaluable. Independent scenarios must not be summed as one portfolio. No orders or live strategy promotion. Original October experiments and reports remain unchanged.
